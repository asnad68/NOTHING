EARLY BUSINESS PILOT

Give your business one identity that machines can inspect.

VERQIVIA is an experimental identity and verification infrastructure. A pilot tests the model with a real organization, using a limited set of claims, evidence and verification procedures before wider integration.

WHAT A PILOT IS

A controlled test, not a certification.

VERQIVIA does not replace company registration, professional advice, regulatory authorization or domain ownership systems. A pilot evaluates whether a transparent identity-and-verification layer is useful for your workflows.

START A CONVERSATION

Bring one real verification problem.

Good pilots begin with one concrete problem: proving which domain is official, showing who may act for a business, or making a business identity easier for software to inspect.
